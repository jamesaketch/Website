Research

Undergraduate thesis2024 to 2026
UCD School of Economics, supervised by Prof. Eoghan O'Neill

Housing Like Irish Weather? Volatility Regimes and Asymmetric Transmission in the Irish Housing Market

Develops a Bayesian Markov-switching VAR with a novel Student-t shrinkage prior, the 𝔍𝔄 prior, which nests the Minnesota prior and estimates tail thickness jointly with the coefficients. Regimes are identified from demand-side dynamics rather than house prices.

A differentiable Hamilton filter inside NUTS carries regime uncertainty through every stage. Finds a 28-quarter asset-market stress episode (2008Q2 to 2015Q1) and a credit response roughly twice as large under high volatility.
