Is Housing Like Irish Weather?

Undergraduate thesis, UCD School of Economics. Supervised by Prof. Eoghan O'Neill.

Volatility regimes and asymmetric transmission in the Irish housing market.

The model. A three-stage hierarchical Bayesian MS-VAR(X) across demand, supply, credit, asset and residential property price blocks, under two latent volatility regimes.

The JA Prior. A hierarchical Student-t shrinkage prior that nests the Minnesota prior as a limiting case, with shrinkage, lag decay and tail thickness estimated from the data.

The inference. A differentiable Hamilton filter inside NUTS marginalises the discrete regimes, addressing generated-regressor bias across the modelling stages.
